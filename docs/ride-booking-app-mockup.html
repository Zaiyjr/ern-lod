Light & Airy Theme Uber / Airbnb Modern Aesthetic

Light & Airy Design System — Clean Scandinavian/Swiss minimalism, Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings) & Inter (Body), accent #2563EB, slate base #0F172A, 8pt spacing rhythm, 44–54pt accessible touch targets, 32px bottom-sheet border radii with ambient diffusion shadows.

Interactive Prototype Wiring — Tap primary buttons to step forward (01→02→03→04→05), back button returns to prior screen, and finishing screen loops seamlessly back to start. Left and right arrow keys also navigate between screens.
